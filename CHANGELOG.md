## [v0.2.0] - 2026-10-06

### Highlights

v0.2.0 adds a durable ledger with projections for documents, vocabularies, source connectors, and media, plus a console that surfaces inspect, access, and activity feeds. It introduces cross-SDK behavioural contracts and native bindings for C, Python, TypeScript, and Swift, alongside signed archives, mTLS clients, and authenticated gRPC/REST. Ingestion expands through the remember verb with source deliveries, repository snapshots, playbooks, and document chunking, while consolidation gains budgeted NREM/REM phases, provider admission gates, and bounded concurrency. Retrieval adds graph recall, relation ranking, alignment boost, and checkpointed semantic recall with continuity and crash recovery.

- remember gains source_delivery, source_sync, document, vocabulary, repository_snapshot, and derive objects.
- console adds inspect, access, activity feed, media catalog, and domain-profile views.
- cross-SDK contract with Python leg, C ABI (hm-capi), Swift package, and TypeScript SDK.
- hm archive pack/unpack, playbook import, and provider admission gate on reconstruction and consolidation.
- budgeted REM consolidation, graph recall, relation ranking, and alignment boost.
